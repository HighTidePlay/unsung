# Unsung for Mac

Unsung splits your service music into a vocal channel and a music channel, so you can fade the lead vocal in and out live, turn any song into an instrumental for pre-service music and prayer moments, and send music and vocals to your console on separate channels (works with Dante Virtual Soundcard).

Website: https://unsung.hightideplay.com

## Download

Get Unsung.dmg from the latest release on the right, open it, and drag Unsung into your Applications folder. Try it free for 7 days, then unlock it for $19, once.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Apple silicon recommended.
